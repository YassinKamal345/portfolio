NoSub — self-hosted infrastructure, no subscriptions

A fictional company selling a mini-PC "box" so customers run their own services at home with zero subscriptions: AdGuard, in-house apps and a WireGuard VPN back to HQ. We built the headquarters as a lab on Proxmox, in an isolated virtual network.

  • OPNsense firewall with 6 VLANs, DNS, DHCP and a reverse proxy
  • PostgreSQL, Gitea and monitoring with Grafana + Uptime Kuma
  • Everything deployed with Ansible (nosub-infra) — rebuilt from scratch in minutes
  • Hardening & closure: TOTP on OPNsense, risk analysis, a power-outage drill and a GNS3 rehearsal
